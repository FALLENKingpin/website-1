About M&M Dayhome

Welcome to M&M Dayhome! Founded in 2015, M&M Dayhome was born from a personal miracle. When my daughter Megan was two and struggling to speak, I opened a play-based dayhome, believing that friendship would help her find her voice. Within just one month of active peer play, her speech completely bloomed! Today, our dayhome is proudly named after my two children, Megan and Matthew. They are the reason I wake up every single morning excited to work, support our community, and inspire others. We know firsthand that play is essential for healthy child development. It constructs critical brain pathways, nurtures deep social skills, and unlocks physical potential. We bring this exact heart-centered, science-backed approach to your children every single day.
